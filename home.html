Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how CalcDaily guarantees numerical precision, protects client data privacy, and determines formula standards.

Are my inputs kept completely private?

Yes. All arithmetic and date calculations run in your browser. Your figures and dates are not transmitted to our servers. On business-day tools, choosing a country loads a static holiday list for that country; inputs are not stored or put into the URL.

How does the Days Between Dates tool handle leap years?

Our date logic adopts the Gregorian calendar standard, fully factoring in leap years (years divisible by 4, except century years not divisible by 400). Calculations use local calendar dates only — there is no timezone conversion to UTC — so you avoid the off-by-one-day errors that UTC conversion can introduce around daylight saving boundaries.

What formulas will the financial and loan calculators use?

The Loan Calculator uses the standard reducing-balance amortization formula M = P[r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1]. The Compound Interest Calculator estimates future value with compounding and optional contributions. The Savings Goal Calculator finds a monthly amount or timeline for a target.

Is CalcDaily free for commercial and educational use?

Yes. Every tool on CalcDaily (14 free calculators) is permanently unrestricted and free for students, engineers, financial planners, and researchers without any subscription barriers or account signups.
